# Nelio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Nelio is a private couples app for sharing quiet signals, notes, presence, and
shared countdowns. This policy explains what information the Nelio iOS app
uses and why.

## Information We Use

### Account information

Nelio uses Sign in with Apple. Apple and our authentication provider, Supabase,
may provide and store an Apple account identifier and an email address,
including Apple's private relay address when that option is selected. We use
this information to create and maintain your account and sign you in.

### Profile and relationship information

If you provide it, Nelio stores your display name, time zone, profile photo,
relationship type, anniversary or other shared countdown dates, and pairing
information. You and your partner can also share presence status, short notes,
and one-tap signals.

### Optional coarse location

Nelio does not request GPS, precise location, or background location access.
You may optionally enter a city or approximate location manually. If provided,
the approximate coordinates are used to support coarse distance and presence
features. Nelio does not continuously track your movement.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple provides Nelio with a device notification
token. We use it to deliver partner signals and relevant app notifications.
You can change notification permission in iOS Settings. The token is removed
from our account when you sign out where the app can do so.

### Onboarding analytics

If you answer optional onboarding questions, Nelio may record the selected
answer for product analysis, including identity, age range, relationship
duration, and notification-permission outcome. These events are buffered on the
device before sign-in and are associated with your account only when they are
uploaded after sign-in. Skipped questions are not recorded.

### Purchases

Nelio uses RevenueCat and Apple's App Store services to process and restore
subscriptions. Nelio receives subscription and entitlement information needed
to provide premium features. Payment card details are handled by Apple and are
not stored by Nelio.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide account, pairing, presence, signal, note, countdown, widget, and
  notification features;
- authenticate users and protect access to partner data;
- provide and restore subscriptions and premium entitlements;
- understand onboarding completion and improve the product; and
- respond to support requests and maintain the service.

We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

## Service Providers

Nelio relies on the following providers to operate the app:

- Apple, for Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions, and push
  notifications;
- Supabase, for authentication, database storage, file storage, and realtime
  synchronization; and
- RevenueCat, for subscription entitlement management.

These providers process information on Nelio's behalf or as required to
provide their services. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

## Retention and Deletion

We retain account and shared relationship information while your account is
active or as needed to provide the service. You can request account deletion
from inside the app using the account deletion control. Deleting an account
removes the account and user-linked profile, presence, signal, pairing, device
token, and analytics records according to the database deletion rules. Shared
couple or countdown records that belong to both partners may remain for the
remaining partner, with your creator attribution removed where applicable.

Some local settings and widget data may remain on your device until the app is
deleted or those settings are reset by iOS.

## Security

Nelio uses authenticated access controls and row-level security for its
Supabase data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take
reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access.

## Children's Privacy

Nelio is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The
updated version will be published at this URL with a new update date.
